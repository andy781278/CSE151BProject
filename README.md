I fine-tuned DeBERTa-v3-large to answer multiple-choice science questions from the AI2 Reasoning Challenge. Adding BM25 retrieval of 10 supporting passages per question raised mean test accuracy by less than 1 point, and accuracy varied by up to 3.8 points between random seeds, so the gain was within noise. I added a cross-encoder that reranks BM25’s top 20 passages before keeping 10. Across 6 seeds, mean accuracy rose from 72.3% to 74.0%, the improvement was statistically significant (t ≈ 3.2), and the spread between seeds dropped from 3.8 to 1.4 points.
